    How it works

    Two movies are similar when the same people watched both. The model uses no genres or descriptions, only what 610 people actually watched. Each movie keeps its 50 closest neighbors, and the movies you pick vote for theirs.

    How well it works

    I hid 20% of each person's ratings, trained on the rest, and checked how often the top 10 contained a movie they loved.
